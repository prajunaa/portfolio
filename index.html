2026
Dataset size, augmentation, and what a CNN actually learns
Sole author, peer-reviewed in The National High School Journal of Science.
  • Fine-tuned ResNet-18 to measure how dataset size and augmentation reshape a network’s internal features — not just its accuracy.
  • Ten times more real data doubled class separation; accuracy rose from 82.9% to 88.3%.
  • Augmentation cut within-class spread by up to 26% but never reliably separated the classes: it helps when data is scarce, it doesn’t replace data.
  • Every experiment run independently on my own machine.

What I’m building toward

Focalect can tell an owner which ads drive sales. It can’t tell them why a shopper walks past one shelf and pauses at another.

I want to build affordable vision robots that read that behavior from movement rather than faces, so privacy survives the measurement. The hard part, and the reason I want it: making a robot understand a crowded, constantly changing scene from very little training data.
